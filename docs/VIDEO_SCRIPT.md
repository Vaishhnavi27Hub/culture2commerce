# Demo video script (2 to 3 minutes, screen recording)

**Setup**
- Browser window about 1280 wide, zoom 100%, bookmarks bar hidden.
- `npm run dev` running; badge at top right reads **Live Qloo data**.
- Do one practice run first. Close other tabs and notifications.
- Record screen and microphone. Speak slowly; pause one second after each click.

Read the numbers that are on your screen. The ones here are from 13 Oct 2026.

| # | Time | Click or show | Say |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00 | Input screen, top of page. | "Hi, we are Team VKY: Vaishhnavi, Khushi and Yashi. This is Culture2Commerce, an agent that helps first-time entrepreneurs find one small business idea worth testing." |
| 2 | 0:12 | Hover the three "How it works" cards. | "Students and first-timers often have a small budget and a few ideas. Generic AI gives pros and cons for each. We use Qloo's taste graph to recommend one concept, one locality, one audience and one pilot." |
| 3 | 0:28 | Click **Try Nisha's example**. | "Here is Nisha in Bengaluru: ₹15,000, two weekends, choosing between a thrift stall, art prints and a coffee pop-up." |
| 4 | 0:36 | Watch the agents run. Do not click. | "Four agents run in order. The first two call the Qloo API live." |
| 5 | 0:48 | Expand **Local Researcher**. Scroll its Qloo calls. | "The Local Researcher fetches the most popular places in each locality from Qloo, plus existing competitors. You can see each call and its parameters." |
| 6 | 1:02 | Collapse it. Expand **Cultural Opportunity Finder**. | "The Cultural Opportunity Finder uses those places as a taste signal. It asks Qloo how well each idea fits that audience, and pulls brand affinities and demographics." |
| 7 | 1:16 | Click **See the recommendation**. | "The result is one specific bet, with a score and a confidence level." |
| 8 | 1:24 | Point at the four tiles: Concept, Locality, Target audience, Pilot experiment. | "Concept, locality, target audience and pilot experiment." |
| 9 | 1:32 | Scroll to **Without Qloo vs With Qloo**. Click **Without Qloo**, then **With Qloo**, then **Side by side**. | "Without Qloo you get 'it depends'. With Qloo you get a pick backed by real places and brands that this audience already likes." |
| 10 | 1:50 | Scroll to **All options, ranked**. Click **Show all 9 options**. Click a rejected coffee option. | "Coffee has the strongest cultural fit, but it is rejected because it costs about twice the budget. Every rejection shows its reason." |
| 11 | 2:05 | Scroll to **How the final score is computed**, then the confidence panel, then the evidence trail. | "Scoring is a transparent formula. Confidence is about data quality, not a promise. And every piece of evidence is labelled live Qloo data or estimate." |
| 12 | 2:20 | Scroll to **How the answer changes by locality**. | "The same inputs give different answers in different localities." |
| 13 | 2:28 | Click **See the pilot plan**. Scroll slowly. | "The Pilot Planner produces quantities, prices, a schedule, a budget and a continue, pivot or stop rule. It exports to Markdown, PDF or JSON." |
| 14 | 2:42 | In **Report pilot results** type 40, 10000 and 2500. Click **Get verdict**. | "After the pilot, Nisha enters her real numbers and sees predicted versus actual with a verdict." |
| 15 | 2:52 | Scroll to the footer. | "Culture2Commerce: from cultural signal to a tested business idea. Thank you." |

## Tips

- If the badge shows a fallback, mention it in step 4: "the app labels fallback data honestly".
- Keep the cursor still while speaking.
- Aim for under three minutes; steps 12 and 13 can be shortened first.
